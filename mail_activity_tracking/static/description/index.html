Mail Activity Tracking for Odoo

Never wonder whether your important notifications reached their recipients. Techvoot's Mail Activity Tracking provides real-time email notification tracking directly in Odoo chatter, displaying clear status indicators beside each recipient so users can quickly monitor message delivery and engagement.

Overview

Odoo chatter makes communication easy, but it does not always provide clear visibility into the delivery status of email notifications. Users often have no way to quickly determine whether a notification was successfully sent, delivered, opened, or encountered an issue.

Mail Activity Tracking solves this challenge by displaying intuitive email notification tracking statuses directly within the chatter. Each notified recipient is shown with a visual status indicator, allowing users to instantly monitor message delivery, improve communication transparency, and ensure important notifications reach their intended recipients. Whether you manage internal teams or customer communications, it works seamlessly for both Community and Enterprise versions, including Odoo.sh.

Why Businesses Choose This Odoo Mail Activity Tracking Module

Email notifications are a critical part of daily business operations in Odoo. However, standard Odoo provides limited visibility into whether recipients actually receive or interact with those notifications. This can lead to missed communications, delayed follow-ups, and uncertainty when managing customer or internal interactions.

Techvoot's Mail Activity Tracking enhances Odoo chatter by providing clear, recipient-level email tracking information directly within the interface. Users can instantly identify notification delivery statuses through intuitive visual indicators, improving accountability, communication efficiency, and overall user experience.
